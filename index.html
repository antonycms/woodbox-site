Here is the query. Review the SQL before running it.

  1. DATE_TRUNC

    Groups dates by month, so sales on different days share one row.

  2. WHERE

    Includes only paid orders from the start of two months ago onward.

  3. SUM + GROUP BY

    Adds up each month’s revenue and sorts the months in chronological order.
